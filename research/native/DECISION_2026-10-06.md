## 2. The waiver

**What is waived:** G5 (`PREREG_E15.md` §9.8: "all 8 K12b rows within T") for the
adoption of `e15_new` in E15-B, and nothing else.

**The miss, as read** (`data/e15/compare_report.json`):

| slash row | real (target) | `e15_new` | difference | T | `e12_d1p` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| benign control P0Q1 (`host?query`) | 0.99551 | 0.99859 | +0.00309 | 0.00244 | 0.99771 (inside) |

- **D1 holds.** Of the "slash" plans that kept their query, 14,875 of 14,876 carry the
  slash (`e12_d1p`: 14,844 of 14,845). None of the 20 "no slash" plans that kept theirs
  carries one.
- **The miss comes from 71 "no slash" plans** (of 15,000): `e15_new` kept the query on
  20 of them, `e12_d1p` on 33. The plans that drop their query leave the host+query
  pool, which is then nearly all slashed.
- It is a query-honouring effect on a rare plan, not a slash-placement error. It is
  probably not chance (z ≈ 2.3).

**What the waiver does not change:**
- **G5's rule and T** stay as registered for every later experiment. A later arm judged
  against `e15_new` is held to all 8 rows within T.
- **E3 is unchanged.** It requires the 8 slash rows within T (`DECISION_2026-09-29.md`
  §3), and is read on the model as it is. On `e15_new` this row counts as missed. Only
  an explicit decision by the owner could change that, and none has been taken.
