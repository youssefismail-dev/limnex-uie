# Owner decision, 2026-09-28: the baseline after E9

**[DECISION]** This is an owner decision, not an empirical claim.
- It was taken after E9's reading (`E9_RESULTS.md`, a9e1932).
- It changes no registered result, criterion or threshold.
- The registered documents keep their text; their status lines point here.

## 1. The decisions

1. **`e9_ln` with the raw decode is the new baseline.**
   - `e9_ln` is the official objective and model baseline from now on.
   - The raw decode is the official baseline decoding configuration.
   - Two things are no longer the production or research baseline:
     - the old objective, E7's and `e9_base`'s per-batch mean;
     - the frozen PTH decode-time delta.
   - The adoption is not reopened unless a concrete reproducibility or integrity
     problem is found.
2. **E8 is permanently unread and obsolete.**
   - E9 directly addressed the bias E8 was measuring: path and query emission
     distorted by the objective.
   - No further research budget goes to interpreting, extending or promoting E8.
   - Its artifacts and history are kept for provenance. It is not a candidate and
     not a baseline.
3. **The remaining query-emission gap is the active research question**
   (`research/qgap/QG_PLAN.md`).
   - Evidence comes first: decompose the gap into measurable mechanisms before any
     fix.
   - Every experiment states its hypothesis, prediction, criterion and decision
     rule before it runs.
   - Diagnostics stay separate from the baseline and never change it.

**What does not change, and why:**
- **Library defaults.**
  - `TrainConfig.loss_norm` stays `"batch"`, and `generate`'s defaults stay as
    they were, so every earlier recipe and run still reproduces byte for byte.
  - The baseline is named explicitly in `uie.baseline`, never reached by changing
    a default.
- **`uie/free.py`** (E7 + the frozen delta + DC1, adopted 2026-09-26) stays bound
  to E7's weights. It reproduces the published runs and is no longer the baseline.
- **`uie.controlled.generate_controlled`** (SC1's held resampling) is a reference
  instrument under the native principle (`NATIVE_REPAIR_PLAN.md` §0). The
  baseline's control mode keeps SC1's state tables and does not resample.
- **Frozen records.** These stay as published:
  - E5, R4, the E3 report;
  - gate v2 and its thresholds, and every instrument;
  - every published result. Earlier results keep their resting decode, the
    frozen delta; the delta is retired for the baseline only.
