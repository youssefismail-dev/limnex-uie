### 5.2 benign P1Q1 PTH — checked, and CLOSED

`research/e18/PTH_CHECK_RESULT.md` (tool `research/e18/pth_check.py`, record
`data/e18/pth_check.json`). Read-only; nothing registered or trained.

- **No enforceable structural violation.** On every well-formedness property where real
  paths are at 0 (bad percent-encoding, non-ASCII, whitespace, double slashes, unseen
  characters, over-length, over-depth), the model is also at 0. Any-violation: generated
  0.313% against real 0.231%, worth about 0.0008 of a 0.007 gap.
- **A feature-independent scan agrees:** paths containing a character 4-gram unseen in
  185,049 real training paths — generated 29.99%, real held-out control 28.65%.
- The one real plan violation (path dropped under P1, 0.19%) **cannot move this cell**:
  `discriminate_with_floor` excludes URLs whose field is empty, so a path-less URL is not
  in the PTH comparison.
- What remains is a matter of degree (paths ~10% shorter, slightly shallower) — D2's
  territory, tried on this exact cell in E13 (−0.0018) and closed.

**Closed, per the owner's instruction. The cell stands at 0.0539 CONVICTED.**
