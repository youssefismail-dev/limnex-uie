## 1. The baseline: `e12_final` replaces `e11_plan`

**The question, in the owner's words.** "I want a factual baseline decision, not
simply 'E12 got closer to E3.' Consider validity, remaining detector gaps,
regressions, copying/novelty, reproducibility, and whether E12 introduced any
undesirable behavior."

**The method.**
- Each comparison is made two ways:
  - against `e12_base`, E11's recipe retrained in the same session from the same
    start. This is the controlled comparison;
  - against E11's `e11_plan` at step 20,000. This one crosses sessions.
- Two runs of the same recipe differ by up to 0.0148 on free cells and 0.0078 on
  control cells (`e12_base` against `e11_plan`). A cross-session difference smaller
  than that is not evidence.

| question | finding |
|---|---|
| **Validity** | Equal. Both read VERIFY PASS. E12 adds byte identity through the slash plan's draws in every arm and mode, and a scorer exact against E7's published cells |
| **Detector gaps** | No measurable change from the levers. Same session, `e12_final` differs from `e12_base` by at most 0.0079 on any gated cell, under the 0.0141 allowance. The verdict tally is 26 CLEARED / 5 UNDECIDED / 1 CONVICTED, against E11's 25 / 6 / 1; the difference is within noise |
| **Regressions** | None beyond the allowance (K12c). Phishing free QRY reads +0.1141, against E11's +0.0953, but the same-session control reads +0.1101. That is a session effect, and E11's value was a favourable draw |
| **The slash rows** | **A real, replicated improvement**, the only one on an E3 component. In both slash-plan arms, each of E11's three failing rows moves closer to the real rate. `e12_final` against `e12_base`: +0.26 against +0.40, −0.33 against −0.48, −0.24 against −0.44, which is 31–45% closer; `e12_no_r1` is 37–59% closer. None reaches tolerance |
| **Honouring** | Benign host-route queries honour the requested state better: 99.16% against 98.35% (z +6.3). Phishing path-plus-query is worse: 96.02% against 97.06% (z −2.8), a state the gate cannot measure, and only in the R1 arm (`e12_no_r1`: 97.26%) |
| **Copying** | **Moved further below the natural rate**, significantly: free 16.86% against 20.83% (z −4.8); control 21.14% against 24.03% (z −9.7). The most common real query strings (seen 20+ times in training) are produced even less often: phishing free 14.64% against 18.62%, where real URLs are at 26.31%. Not a registered failure: K12d reads UNDER as reported, and no memorisation flag fires. But under `DECISION_2026-09-30.md` §4.3, lower is not better, and this moves away from the real distribution |
| **Novelty** | Unchanged (K12d) |
| **New behaviour** | The slash plan adds a supplied decision the model obeys imperfectly. It writes the slash on 99.6–99.9% of URLs told S = 1, and obeys S = 0 70–97% of the time in the rare states. Without the plan this failure mode does not exist; with it, the slash rows improve |
| **R1** | No measured benefit on any cell. It costs 49,152 parameters, about 6 training minutes, and the 384-token memory peak |
| **Reproducibility** | Generation is byte-reproducible from the checkpoints in both. Training is not bit-reproducible across sessions; that spread is now measured |

**The decision: adopt `e12_final` as the official research baseline**, effective
2026-10-01.
- **Why it is scientifically preferable:**
  - validity is equal;
  - no gated cell regresses;
  - its verdict tally is equal or better;
  - it carries the only demonstrated, replicated progress on an E3 component (the
    slash rows);
  - QC1 licensed no native route to those rows.
- **Why R1 stays in it:** the measured arm without R1 (`e12_no_r1`) fails five
  slash rows, against `e12_final`'s three. That difference is not attributable,
  but adopting the arm that measured worse cannot be called preferable. R1 is
  flagged as a simplification candidate: E13's registration states whether it is
  kept.
- **What the adoption carries into E13 as named diagnosis targets:**
  1. copying moved further below the natural rate;
  2. the phishing path-plus-query honouring drop in the R1 arm;
  3. imperfect S obedience;
  4. phishing free QRY's true level is about 0.11, not 0.0953.
- **What it does not mean:**
  - E12 met its criteria. It did not, and no arm is a candidate.
  - E11's record changes. It does not: E11 stays as registered.
  - The library's `uie/baseline.py` changes. It is still bound to `e9_ln`'s
    weights; binding a new baseline there is a separate step for the owner to
    order.

## 2. E13: the owner's directive

> "Proceed with E13 as a new, focused research phase. Start with a new
> registration/preregistration and diagnose the remaining E3 failures before
> proposing any training change."

**Focus (the owner's list):**
- phishing free-mode QRY content, especially the large remaining gap;
- the remaining benign control QRY / PTH / SUB cells;
- the remaining phishing control PTH cell;
- the remaining slash-obedience failures.

**Rules (the owner's):**
- "Do not start with a guessed fix. Let the diagnosis determine the next lever."
- "One justified change at a time, explicit criteria, validity checks,
  reproducibility, and no open-ended tuning cycle."
- "The objective of E13 is still the same: make the smallest scientifically
  justified set of changes necessary to address the remaining failures and, if
  the evidence supports it, reach the E3 finish line."
- **Data is now in scope:**

  > "You are explicitly authorized to investigate the data itself in E13. If the E13
  > diagnosis provides evidence that the remaining QRY/content failures—or any other
  > remaining E3 failures—are caused or materially constrained by the training
  > data/corpus rather than the model architecture or decoding, you may introduce new
  > benign and/or phishing data as scientifically justified."

  New data, if any, must document all of the following:
  - the deficiency found;
  - why it explains the failure;
  - what data is needed and why;
  - its sourcing and quality control;
  - how leakage, duplication and contamination are prevented;
  - how it is preregistered and evaluated.

  Then: "Do not add data merely because E3 failed."
- **Stop point:** "Do not begin final Kaggle training until the E13 diagnosis,
  proposed intervention, and preregistration are complete and presented for
  approval."

**Standing constraints that still apply:**
- the gate, its thresholds and the tolerance standard stay frozen;
- no optimizing the generator against the metric;
- no rejection, resampling or masking as the core mechanism;
- no capacity, extra steps or decoder changes just to improve a result;
- the closed phishing free PTH attribution branch stays closed. That cell is now
  CLEARED in the baseline; the phishing control PTH cell the owner names is a
  different cell, diagnosed in E13 without rerunning that branch's ladder.

**Order:**
1. Register E13's diagnosis before any diagnostic code.
2. Run the diagnosis on the laptop.
3. Read it.
4. Propose the intervention the evidence licenses.
5. Preregister E13's training.
6. Present everything to the owner for approval.
