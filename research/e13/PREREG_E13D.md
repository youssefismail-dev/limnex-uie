- **Registered:** 2026-10-01, before any E13 code, measurement or training.
- **Decided by:**
  - **The owner** (`DECISION_2026-10-01.md` §2): E13 diagnoses before it proposes
    any training change. Data is in scope, but only with a defensible data-related
    cause.
  - **The executor:** the readings and rules below.
- **Baseline:** `e12_final` (`DECISION_2026-10-01.md` §1). `e12_base` is the
  replication arm.
- **Material:** E12's registered files (`data/e12/gen`, VERIFY PASS), the
  `e12_final` checkpoint, and the data bundle. Laptop only: nothing is trained or
  generated by the model, and nothing reaches Kaggle.
- **Frozen, unchanged:** the gate v2, its thresholds and the tolerance standard;

  path-body ablation ladder is not rerun.

## 0. In plain words

Six gated cells and three slash rows stand between `e12_final` and E3. For each,
E13-D asks where the failure lives before anything is changed:

1. **Is the training data the same population the gate tests against?** If real
   training URLs are themselves distinguishable from the real validation URLs the
   gate uses, no model can pass by learning its data better. The cause is data.
2. **Does the model reproduce its own training data?** If generated URLs are
   distinguishable from the real training URLs they were learned from, the model
   has not learned its data. The cause is learning.
3. **Is there enough data?** Phishing has about 8,300 domain-frame query examples
   against benign's 57,800. Two signs would say data is the limit:
   - real query templates keep appearing as the data grows;
   - a simple learner still improves at full data size.
4. **How far is each near-bar cell from clearing reliably?** Four runs of the same
   family already exist.
5. **Why does the slash plan fail to be obeyed?** A probe asks the model directly,
   one decision at a time: are its misses spread evenly (a general sharpness
   limit), or concentrated where training gave it few examples (a data limit)?
6. **What caused E12's side effects?** Lower copying, and the phishing
   path-plus-query honouring dip. These are described, not licensed.

## 2. Readings and rules

All detector readings use QC1's harness (`qc1_common.harness`) with the gate's
detector:
- D = 12 seeds (20260806 + i);
- three disjoint draws of the reference population per seed;
- the floor is AUC(draw a, draw b);
- the excess is AUC(draw c, compared set) − floor;
- n is min(1,800, a third of the smaller pool, the compared set's size);
- t_crit is t(0.975, 11) = 2.2010.

Below, x_GV is the harness's excess for generated (`e12_final`) against real
validation. It is the gate's quantity, re-measured (V2).

**DX1 — the population (data shift).** Per cell:
- **Measure:** x_TV, the excess of the real training twin against real
  validation. The floor comes from validation.
- **Rule:**
  - **SHIFT-DOMINANT:** mean x_TV ≥ 0.5 · mean x_GV, with one-sample t over the
    seeds > t_crit;
  - **SHIFT-PRESENT:** significant and ≥ 0.25 · x_GV;
  - **NO SHIFT:** otherwise.
- No replication is needed: both sides are real.

**DX2 — learning (does the model reproduce its training data?).** Per cell:
- **Measure:** x_GT, the excess of generated against the real training twin. Here
  the training twin supplies the floor's draws, a, b and c.
- **Rule:**
  - **LEARNING-GAP:** mean x_GT ≥ 0.5 · mean x_GV, with t > t_crit;
  - **NO GAP:** otherwise.
- **Replication:** on `e12_base`. The reading counts only if both arms agree.

**Attribution per cell:**

| DX1 | DX2 | attribution |
|---|---|---|
| SHIFT-DOMINANT | NO GAP | **DATA (population)** |
| NO SHIFT | LEARNING-GAP | **LEARNING** |
| SHIFT-DOMINANT or SHIFT-PRESENT | LEARNING-GAP | **BOTH** |
| any other combination | | **UNRESOLVED** at this power |

**DX3 — quantity (is there enough data?).** For the three query cells, with free
benign QRY as the reference:
- **Coverage.** What share of the real validation pool's query *templates* occur
  in a random fraction f of the training twin, for f = 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1 (seeded)?
  - A template is the ordered parameter keys with each value replaced by its
    character class (letters, digits, hex, other); the email-like and UUID-like
    values are classes too.
  - g is the gain from the last doubling: cov(1) − cov(1/2).
- **A learner's curve.** A character n-gram model (order 6, stupid back-off,
  seeded sampling) is trained on the same fractions of the training twin's
  queries. It generates the harness's n queries per seed; x_NG(f) is their excess
  against real validation, with D = 12.
  - s is the last doubling's improvement: mean x_NG(1/2) − mean x_NG(1), with
    paired t over the seeds.
  - The learner is a proxy. It shows whether this distribution, for a learner,
    still rewards more data at today's size. It does not stand for the
    transformer's own curve.
- **Rule:** **DATA-LIMITED** only if both hold:
  - g ≥ 0.02;
  - s ≥ 0.01 with paired t > t_crit.

  **NOT DATA-LIMITED** otherwise. The reference cell is reported beside each.

**DX4 — distance to a reliable clear (descriptive).** For each remaining gated cell,
across the four readings of the family (E11 `e11_plan`, `e12_base`, `e12_final`,
`e12_no_r1`):
- the mean x and the between-run SD;
- P(CLEARED) for a new run at that mean with `e12_final`'s standard error;
- the x needed for P(CLEARED) ≥ 0.8.

Normal approximation over the gate's own rule. **Licenses nothing.**

**DX5 — slash obedience (teacher-forced probe on `e12_final`).**
- **The decision point:** for each probe URL, the model is fed its class, frame,
  suffix and supplied plan (P, Q, S), then the URL up to the end of the host. The
  probe records the probability of the next token that violates the supplied S:
  - for S = 1: anything but a path start or `<RS>`;
  - for S = 0: a path start or `<RS>`.
- **Populations:** real `name_val` URLs, every one up to 3,000 per population:
  - phishing P0Q0 (S = 1);
  - phishing P0Q1 (S = 1);
  - benign P0Q1 with and without the slash;
  - benign P0Q0 with and without the slash.
- **Measure:**
  - m, the mean violation probability;
  - c, the share of the total violation mass held by the top 10% of URLs;
  - the training support of the top decile's (suffix, state, S) contexts, beside
    the population's.
- **Rule:**
  - **UNIFORM:** c < 0.30. The misses are a general sharpness of the decision.
  - **CONCENTRATED:** c ≥ 0.50, and the top decile's median training support is
    below a tenth of the population's. The misses sit where data is thin.
  - **MIXED:** otherwise.

**DX6 — E12's side effects (descriptive, licenses nothing):**
- the copying shift, `e12_final` against `e12_base`, by frequency band, suffix
  and query length;
- the phishing P1Q1 honouring dip, by failure type, in all three E12 arms.

## 7. Amendment 1 (2026-10-01): DX5 probes the model's own URLs

**Made after the dry run and before any real E13-D measurement.**

**What the dry run showed.** V5 failed at toy size. My structure check of the dry
output printed that one validity line. No other dry value was read, and the dry
files remain unread.

**Why V5 was mis-specified.** As registered, V5 compares two different
populations:
- the probe runs on **real** validation hosts;
- the miss rate it must predict comes from the model's **own generated** hosts.

V5 therefore tests whether the two host populations differ, not whether the probe
is right. A probe that is correct can fail it.

**The amendment:**
1. **DX5 is measured on `e12_final`'s own generated URLs, where the misses happen.**
   - Populations:
     - control mode: phishing P0Q0 and P0Q1, benign P0Q1 and P0Q0, each split by
       the supplied S;
     - free mode: phishing bare hosts, P0Q0 with S = 1 (the free_bare row).
   - Each URL's supplied state is rebuilt from the seeds, as E12's S honouring
     did (`e12_score.state_positions`).
   - Each URL is fed with the plan it was generated under: the requested P and Q,
     the supplied S, its own suffix, and the domain frame.
   - Every URL of each population is probed. There is no 3,000 cap here.
   - The DX5 rule (UNIFORM / CONCENTRATED / MIXED) applies to these populations.
2. **V5 becomes the probe's own check.** In each generated population, the probe's
   mean violation probability must lie within the 95% Wilson interval of that
   population's observed violation rate. A correct probe passes it.
3. **The registered real-URL populations are still measured, and reported beside:**
   - their mean violation probability;
   - the real-against-generated difference. This says whether the misses live in
     the model's own hosts.

   They license nothing.

## 8. Amendment 2 (2026-10-01): DX7, under-fitting or sampling

**Registered after the E13-D reading and before any DX7 measurement.**

**Why it is needed.** The reading (`E13D_RESULTS.md`) licenses a learning-side lever
in all six cells, and §3 requires the specific lever to be justified on its own.
Two mechanisms fit "the model has not learned its data", and they need different
levers:
- **under-fitting:** the model assigns too little probability to real field
  strings. More learning signal on those strings would address it;
- **sampling:** the model fits them, but its samples drift. No allowed lever
  addresses that, because decoder changes are excluded.

DX7 separates the two.

**DX7, per cell:** the six cells, plus free benign QRY as the reference.
- **The strings:** the cell's real validation pool (the gate's own, as in DX1).
  Each URL is encoded with its true plan, in the domain frame.
- **The transformer** (`e12_final`, teacher-forced on CPU) gives each string a
  per-character negative log-likelihood. It covers the field's characters plus
  the token that ends the field (the next marker or EOS), divided by characters +
  1. The field's opening marker, which is the presence decision, is excluded. For
  SUB, every subdomain field counts, each with its terminator.
- **The n-gram** is an interpolated Witten–Bell character model, order 6, trained
  on the cell's full training twin (the same strings as DX3's learner). It scores
  the same strings per character, including the end symbol.
  - It sees less data than the transformer did: one frame and one state, with no
    context before the field. That makes the comparison conservative.
- **Measure:** Δ = NLL_transformer − NLL_n-gram per string. Its mean has a
  bootstrap 95% interval (2,000 resamples, seed 20261004).
  - Reported beside it: the transformer's own NLL on a 3,000-string seeded sample
    of the training twin, against validation (its generalisation gap).
- **Rule:**
  - **UNDER-FIT:** mean Δ > 0 and the interval excludes 0. The transformer
    predicts real held-out field strings worse than a context-free n-gram trained
    on fewer of them;
  - **FITS:** mean Δ < 0 and the interval excludes 0;
  - **UNRESOLVED:** otherwise.

**What DX7 licenses** (it refines §3's learning-side licence, for the cells that
licence covers):

| DX7 | licenses |
|---|---|
| UNDER-FIT | raising that field's learning signal within the fixed step budget. The single most targeted form is a loss weight on the field's content tokens; the value and arms are fixed in E13's registration |
| FITS | no training lever from this reading. The gap is in sampling, and decoder changes are excluded |
| UNRESOLVED | nothing |

**Validity:**
- **V6:** the transformer's span decodes to the gate's field string for at least
  99% of each cell's URLs. Mismatches are excluded and counted.
- **V7:** the n-gram's next-character distribution sums to 1 within 1e-9 on 100
  seeded contexts per cell.

**Prediction** [HYPOTHESIS]:
- UNDER-FIT in the small cells: free phishing QRY and control benign P0Q1 QRY.
- FITS or UNRESOLVED where the transformer already matched the 6-gram's samples:
  control benign P1Q1 QRY, and free benign QRY.

## 9. Amendment 3 (2026-10-01): DX8, the generated context or the field's own sampling

**Registered after DX7's reading and before any DX8 measurement.**

**Why it is needed.** DX7 read FITS in all six cells. The transformer predicts real
held-out field strings far better than the n-gram does, yet its samples are worse.
"Sampling" covers two mechanisms, and they need different levers:
- **The context:** the model writes the field after its own earlier tokens (host,
  path), which differ from real ones. DX5 found slash misses twice as likely on
  the model's own hosts. A training-side lever addresses this: training on the
  model's own prefixes.
- **The field's own sampling at temperature 1:** the field's distribution, sampled
  from a real context, still drifts. The lever for this is decoder-side, and
  decoder changes are excluded by the owner's rules.

**DX8, per cell** (the six cells):
- **Samples:** real validation URLs from the cell's pool. Each is teacher-forced
  with its true plan, in the domain frame, up to and including the field's opening
  marker. The model then samples the field, raw at temperature 1.0 as registered,
  until the field ends.
  - For SUB, everything after the label is sampled up to the first non-SUB end, so
    all subdomains come from the model.
  - The seed is 20261005, one sample per URL.
  - URLs too long to encode are skipped and counted.
- **Measure:** x_RC, the harness excess of these real-context samples against real
  validation strings (QC1's harness, D = 12, the floor from validation). It uses
  the same n as DX1, and is paired with x_GV seed for seed.
- **Rule:**
  - **CONTEXT-LED:** mean x_RC ≤ 0.5 · mean x_GV, with the paired difference
    x_GV − x_RC significant (t > t_crit). Real context removes at least half the
    tell;
  - **FIELD-LED:** mean x_RC ≥ 0.8 · mean x_GV, or the difference is not
    significant;
  - **SPLIT:** otherwise.

**What DX8 licenses:**

| DX8 | licenses |
|---|---|
| CONTEXT-LED | a training-side lever on exposure to the model's own context: training on model-written prefixes. The form and arms are fixed in E13's registration |
| FIELD-LED | no lever within the owner's current rules (decoder-side only). Reported to the owner as a decision |
| SPLIT | nothing |

**Validity:**
- **V8:** every sampled field string is non-empty and decodes without an unknown
  token, for at least 98% of each cell's URLs.
- **V9:** x_GV at DX8's n reproduces DX1's within ±0.02 (a seed-for-seed rerun of
  the same draws).

**Prediction** [HYPOTHESIS]: FIELD-LED for the query cells. Temperature lowered the
query tell before (R2's interaction, +0.0351). SPLIT or CONTEXT-LED for the PTH and
SUB cells, which sit after the model's own host.
